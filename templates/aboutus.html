About Tuty Restaurant

Fresh food, warm service, and celebrations made simple.

Tuty Restaurant brings together South Indian flavours, family dining, room stays, and event hospitality in the heart of Tuticorin.

Our Story

Welcome to Tuty Restaurant, a dining destination in Tuticorin where familiar local flavours meet thoughtful hospitality. Our kitchen serves vegetarian and non-vegetarian favourites prepared with care, freshness, and consistency.

We believe a restaurant visit should feel comfortable from the first greeting to the final bite. Whether you are joining us for a family dinner, a quick takeaway, a business meal, or a special occasion, our team focuses on clean service, generous food, and a relaxed atmosphere.

Along with restaurant dining, we support room bookings and private events, making Tuty a convenient choice for food, stays, and celebrations in one place.
